Starts: 0,stride,2*stride on the ORIGINAL per-video sample axis. start_index inclusive; end_index exclusive; last_index=end_index-1. No cross-video windows, compaction, padding or partial terminal windows.
Nominal duration T/10; first-to-last timestamp span (T-1)/10. Original source_frame_index is retained; MP4 frame increments differ from sample-axis increments.
