Reference: existing project HOG+Dlib feature space; not anatomical ground truth.
All 5000 original HOG-selected rectangles were reconstructed with the original pipeline; Dlib float32 EAR/MAR and uint8 ROI composite must exactly match V1 cache before inclusion.
V1 did not save historical bbox coordinates. Fairness is checked through deterministic HOG reconstruction and exact cached feature/ROI agreement, not a comparison to a saved historical bbox.
No YuNet used. Original inclusive HOG rectangle endpoints are passed unchanged to the two landmark paths.
Sampling: seed42, class-balanced, round-robin across videos, EAR/MAR quintile-cell coverage; feature-enriched, not prevalence-weighted.
Train/val source is HOG-valid frames only. This experiment does not establish transfer to HOG-failed YuNet fallback frames.
Fitting and candidate selection use train only (5-fold video-group CV); validation is held out. Constant-median predictor is a sanity baseline, not a calibration recommendation.
Linear scaling cannot recover lost sample ranking; a lower MAE alone does not demonstrate reliable feature alignment.
Huber is residual-weighted IRLS (delta1.345); quantile mapping has linear tail extrapolation, without clipping.
Recommendation criteria are exploratory operational criteria, not clinical/anatomical quality thresholds: Pearson/Spearman>=.7, MAE reduction>=20% vs raw and >=10% vs constant, catastrophic residual rate<=1%.
Catastrophic residual means abs(error)>3*train-reference IQR for reporting only. No production gate or feature threshold applied.
Yaw gate candidates were derived from train yaw quantiles and assessed with train OOF predictions before any validation assessment.
Each candidate gate refits/selects simple calibration maps inside that TRAIN pose subset, then the widest jointly passing gate is eligible for held-out validation.
