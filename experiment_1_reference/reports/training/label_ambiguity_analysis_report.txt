15. Label ambiguity hypothesis evidence
Video F1 improvement is small: A +0.228 +/- 1.604 pp, B +1.223 +/- 0.799 pp; only 5/6 runs improve and A42 declines. Mixed predictions occur in 27.80% (A) and 26.53% (B) of evaluable videos, indicating real model prediction inconsistency, but most videos remain class-consistent. Mean within-video probability std is 0.0683/0.0769 and range is 0.1999/0.2242, with some large outliers. Drowsy same-video late-minus-early probability averages only +2.70/+2.99 pp; this is a modest shift, not a general alert-to-drowsy transition. Wide predicted fraction distributions also include consistently misclassified videos. Taken together, evidence that label granularity mismatch is a major cause is WEAK, not evidence that no local ambiguity exists. Coverage is limited to 289/311 videos; smoothing, model errors and weighting changes remain alternatives.
16. Alternative explanations
Video-level performance improvement alone does not prove sequence-level label noise.
Ensemble/smoothing, 상관된 sequence 예측의 평균, random error cancellation, 모델 자체의 오류로도 같은 현상이 나타날 수 있다.
Sequence 지표는 sequence마다, video 지표는 video마다 동일 가중치를 부여하므로 영상별 sequence 개수와 class 비율의 영향도 달라진다. 평가 대상이 289/311개로 제한되는 선택 편향도 남는다.
Sequence는 중첩되어 독립 표본이 아니다. 3개 seed의 반복도 독립적인 추가 영상이 아니다. Validation으로 checkpoint를 선택했으므로 독립적인 최종 평가가 아니다.
